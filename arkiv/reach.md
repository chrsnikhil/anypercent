# Reach

## Who it is for

Speedrunners of deterministic games. In a deterministic game a run's input log is the run: replay it and you get the
same time. That makes a board where every time was replayed by a checker before it was posted, instead of a board
moderated by people watching videos.

Any% is such a board: a handheld console with five arcade games built for it, whose runs are checked and posted
to Arkiv.

## Where people meet it

- **The web board.** One HTML file that reads Arkiv straight from the browser: every game's board, HUMAN badges, which
  ghosts are listed, "who is just ahead of my time", a record book's profile at `#book=ANY-XXX`, and the exact query
  with a `curl` line to run it yourself. It works on a phone and needs no account. When Arkiv does not answer it shows
  a snapshot and says so.
- **The console at the booth.** Play a run, approve the listing in World App, see it on the board, then race the
  listed ghost just ahead of you. The same board opens on the console's own browser and on your phone.
- **Anyone with a terminal.** The README's query (the listed ghost just ahead of 1:44.83) runs with `curl`, no key.
